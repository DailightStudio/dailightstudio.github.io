RunRun Apocalypse data deletion request

App: RunRun Apocalypse · Developer: Dailight Studio

How to request

  1. Check your Google Play Games display name on the game's settings screen.
  2. Email wjs9280@gmail.com with the subject "RunRun Apocalypse data deletion" and your display name.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your game save data stored on our server (Google Firebase): progress, currency, items, costumes and skills, plus coupon and mailbox records and the Google Play Games player ID linked to them.

What is kept

We keep no other data on our server. Google Play purchase records are managed and retained by Google under its policies. Advertising data is processed by Google AdMob; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
